Global China Economic Opinion Monitor & Economic Securitization Index

A weekly, auto-updating monitor of how global English-language media cover China's economy: issues, countries, narratives, and a security-framing index.

  • Data: GDELT Global Knowledge Graph; weekly headline-level corpus of foreign news on China's economy since 2019 Q4, with a URL-based long series back to 2015.
  • Measures: a security-framing index built from a validated headline coding scheme (starting from the Baele & Sterck securitization lexicon), with 95% intervals, threat/measure sub-indices and robustness checks.
  • Features: weekly / quarterly / annual issue rankings via semantic clustering, country-level agenda contrasts, and 36 narrative trackers in four layers.
  • Operations: fetched, computed and published automatically every Monday; methods, codebook and validation are documented openly.
