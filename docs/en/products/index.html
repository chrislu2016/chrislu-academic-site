Global China Economic Opinion Monitor & Economic Securitization Index

Weekly monitor of how global English-language media cover China's economy.

  • Data: GDELT news headlines, 2019 Q4 onward; URL-based series back to 2015.
  • Index: weekly securitization index with confidence intervals and robustness checks.
  • Analysis: issue rankings, country agendas and 36 narrative trackers.
  • Updated every Monday; methods are public.
