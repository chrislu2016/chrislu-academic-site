Lu Hongcheng Chris Lu

School of Journalism and Communication, Tsinghua University · Ph.D. Student in Journalism and Communication

Lu Hongcheng (Chris Lu) is a Ph.D. student in Journalism and Communication at Tsinghua University, with graduation expected in 2027. His research focuses on human–machine communication, economic communication, international communication, and media industries. He has published 11 articles (2 SSCI, 6 CSSCI, and 2 CSSCI Expanded), including 4 as first author, with work in Feminist Media Studies, Sexualities, Chinese Journal of Journalism & Communication, and Modern Communication; 2 further works are forthcoming. He has presented at AEJMC, ICA, IAMCR, and NCA and received the 2026 AEJMC CTEC Top Student Paper award. He also received the Academic Rising Star Award at the 7th Qihao Awards for Young Scholars in New Media Research (2026).
